WBCHSE Class XI Semester Rules used by this app

  • Semester I and Semester II are the two semesters of Class XI.
  • Semester I is MCQ-based; Semester II uses conventional answer scripts.
  • Class XI Semester I, Semester II, Supplementary, Practical and Project marks are submitted at the end of Semester II.
  • Pass requirement is 30% in the required subjects, with theory and project/practical passed independently.
  • For the supplied 2026 school record: 40-mark theory → pass 12; 35-mark theory → pass 11; 20-mark project → pass 6; 30-mark practical → pass 9.

The mark maxima above are taken from the supplied XI-2026 marks-record PDF. The app does not overwrite a subject's scheme with a generic value.
